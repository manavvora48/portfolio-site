01 // WHAT ARE COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE?

How This Website Uses Browser Storage

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. Alongside cookies, modern web applications use browser localStorage and sessionStorage to keep interactive features working smoothly without sending unnecessary data across the network.

Zero Advertising Trackers: This portfolio does not use any third-party advertising pixels, cross-site tracking cookies, or behavioral profiling scripts.
