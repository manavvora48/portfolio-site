01 // OVERVIEW

Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to the portfolio website of Manav Vora ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share when exploring this website or reaching out via our contact form.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is processed and secured, and how you can exercise your data privacy rights.

02 // INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information You Voluntarily Provide

We do not require account registration or collect personal data passively while you browse projects or play the interactive isometric experience. We only collect personal data when you voluntarily submit the Contact Form:

  • Full Name or Alias: Used to address you when replying to your inquiry.
  • Email Address: Used strictly to respond to your message.
  • Message Content: Any project details, questions, or feedback you choose to write.
03 // AUTOMATED & SECURITY DATA

Browser Storage & Anti-Spam Protection

To keep this website secure against automated bot abuse, Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), and denial-of-service spam, your browser processes limited local security state:

  • Rate-Limiting Timestamps (mv_contact_ts): Stored in temporary sessionStorage to enforce a 60-second cooldown and prevent form-flooding attacks.
  • Cookie Consent Record (mv_cookie_consent_v1): Stored in localStorage and a first-party cookie (mv_cookie_consent) for 180 days to remember your privacy preferences.
  • Optional Functional Preferences: Only stored if you explicitly grant consent to Functional storage in the Cookie Banner or Cookie Policy settings.
04 // THIRD-PARTY PROCESSORS

How Your Contact Messages Are Delivered

When you submit the Contact Form, your message is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to our form delivery processor:

  • Web3Forms / FormSubmit: Processes the form payload solely to forward the email directly to manavvora48@gmail.com. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with advertisers or marketing lists.
  • Google Fonts: Typography assets (Manrope and DM Mono) are loaded from Google Fonts CDN using privacy-preserving referrer policies (strict-origin-when-cross-origin).
05 // DATA SECURITY MEASURES

How We Protect Your Information

This website implements defense-in-depth security controls, including:

  • Strict Content Security Policy (CSP) and HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) headers.
  • Input length restrictions, email header injection stripping, and HTML tag sanitization on all form fields.
  • Clickjacking frame-breakers (X-Frame-Options: DENY) and sandboxed form frames.
06 // YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS & CONTACT

Access, Deletion & Consent Withdrawal

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Request access to or deletion of any email correspondence you have sent to us.
  • Withdraw or modify your cookie/storage consent at any time via our Cookie Policy page.
